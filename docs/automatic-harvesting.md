# Automatic Supply Truck harvesting

New Collector Trucks produced by a Supply Depot begin harvesting automatically after their existing deployment/rally movement finishes and their body is clear of production exits and supply loading or delivery access. A missing, rejected or blocked rally triggers a bounded local clearance move through the existing movement controller. Production still uses its existing paid queue, capsule-safe spawning and blocked-exit retry. Preplaced collectors retain their scene's existing initial orders.

Each truck has a configurable `collection_radius`, defaulting to **24 world units**. A produced truck's collection area is centered on its producing depot's position at deployment; delivering elsewhere does not move that center. An accepted explicit harvest command chooses the clicked cache and centers subsequent local collection on it, allowing the player to deliberately work another area. Candidate cache centers must be within the area, registered, nonempty and reachable at an available loading position. The shortest usable navigation route wins, with stable instance identity breaking ties. The radius filter runs before route queries.

The truck gathers **25 supplies per simulated second**, carries up to **100**, returns to an eligible operational owned HQ or Supply Depot, and unloads over **one simulated second**. Supplies come from the finite cache. The drop-off has no spending balance: unloading credits the owner's existing wallet, even when that wallet was at zero. The existing transfer commits cargo removal and wallet credit before notifications, preserving exactly-once deposits and command-safe callbacks.

When its cache empties or becomes unavailable, the truck delivers any cargo before selecting another suitable source inside its existing collection area. A successful deposit otherwise resumes the current nonempty cache. An unusable collection route can select another local cache; it does not expand the search area. Existing drop-off rules remain: a valid delivery keeps its destination, and an invalidated automatic return permits one alternative before blocking with cargo retained.

With no suitable nearby cache, the truck displays **No nearby supplies**, releases its loading/delivery reservation, and searches for a reachable parking destination clear of production exits and resource access lanes. Parking checks the current position and 16 directions at distances of 2.5, 5 and 7.5 world units, with collision checks and distinct reservations for waiting trucks. If local parking is obstructed, it retains its waiting order and retries; it never teleports. Once stationary, it retries local collection every **two simulated seconds**, reconsidering previously unavailable caches. Navigation synchronization and match freeze preserve the existing movement and timing barriers.

Accepted Move, **X Stop**, explicit harvesting and one-time deposit commands supersede pending deployment automation or current harvesting. Move and Stop preserve cargo and disable automatic retries; a stopped truck stays stopped until another deliberate order. New-truck handoff checks both movement order and harvest generation, so later commands from registration, visibility, movement or deployment callbacks take precedence. Production and harvesting costs, capacity, timing, ownership and death-related cargo loss retain their existing rules.

## Validation

`tests/automatic_harvesting_checks.gd` covers production with zero credits and repeated automatic deposits, partial-load delivery before cache replacement, inaccessible sources, fixed collection boundaries, five waiting trucks sharing a depot, subsequent delivery, retry and viewport X Stop. `tests/automatic_deployment_callback_checks.gd` covers newer Stop, Move and harvesting commands issued synchronously during visibility and rally callbacks, before deployment notification. Both suites are included in `tools/validate-m16.ps1` and can also run individually through `tools/run-godot.ps1` with `--fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/<suite>.gd`.

Existing harvesting, Supply Depot, earned depot integration, production, air production, enemy economy, builder integration and construction suites were also exercised. Their single-cache accounting fixtures explicitly use a zero collection radius; the new suite tests normal-radius reselection. Godot's pre-existing script/resource-cache shutdown leak diagnostics remain observable in the air-production and builder-integration processes, separately from their passing assertions and zero runtime error counts.

The final headless runs total **1,572 passing assertions across ten suites**, including 100 automatic-harvesting and 118 deployment-callback checks. The final full graphical automatic run passes **101 assertions**, including the inspected 1280×720 screenshot at `validation-output/automatic-harvesting/no-nearby-supplies.png`. An earlier graphical run exposed a screenshot-size mismatch in the test harness; applying the existing depot harness's viewport scaling settings resolved it. The observed initial screenshot failure has a clearly labeled summary, and final logs remain in that ignored validation directory.
