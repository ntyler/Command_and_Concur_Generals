Unit 4 ended FAILED at `(31.41587,0,19.78682)`, assigned `(33,0,22)`, after eight recoveries, route time 36 seconds. Complete fresh command acceptance passed. Initial inference: source history gives group generation 4 and unit version 2, but these were not printed. Unit identity 4 is field-local and must be compared with its geometry, assignment and command history; it is not a universal identity across runs.

Follow-up inference, verified against original source and slot geometry: the other 49 units all passed arrival and cannot be only 0.58 apart at their distinct slots. The final minimum pair therefore involved unit 4 and a parked peer. Only occupants of `(31.5,0,19)` or `(31.5,0,20.5)` could supply that contact. Even with full arrival and printed-coordinate uncertainty, the final occupant of the latter slot would obstruct a direct retry toward `(33,0,22)`: maximum possible clearance 0.57670144 versus required 0.58. **The neighbor could have parked after unit 4 failed**, so this final arrangement may be a consequence rather than the cause. The logged 36 seconds is whole-route completion, not an observed unit-4 failure timestamp; the longest overlap pair is unidentified.

Neither observation proves the historical cause. Final diagnostic schema validation adds explicit relative/absolute callback timestamps; existing production and regression files remain unchanged.
